In World Cup semi-finals involving two elite, fatigued teams (both coming through 120-minute knockouts), the totals market often overprices BTTS Yes and Over 2.5 due to public focus on star attackers. Hot/humid conditions amplify the low-scoring dynamic. Model output for England vs Argentina 2026 semi-final flagged value on Under 2.5 @ 1.61 and BTTS No @ -120.

**Why:** Tactical caution in single-elimination late rounds, cumulative fatigue, and weather slow tempo and reduce conversion rates more than markets typically adjust.

**How to apply:** When analyzing future World Cup/Euro knockout matches, apply a fatigue-plus-weather discount to team xG (typically 10-15% for 300+ minutes played) and verify whether totals/BTTS markets have priced it in.
