For 2026/27 Premier League fixtures, these sources surface well in WebSearch summaries (verified during Chelsea vs Hull City R4 intel, 2026-09-11):

- **sportsmole.co.uk** — match preview + dedicated "predicted lineup" and "injury, suspension list" pages per fixture; most detailed team news.
- **picksandparlays.net free-picks pages** — explicit odds line movement with dates (moneyline open vs current, total-line moves), US odds format.
- **footballwhispers.com / footballpredictions.com / eaglepredict.com / sportytrader.com** — prediction pages quoting concrete decimal odds for 1X2, AH (-1.5 etc.), O/U lines, BTTS.
- **espn.com/soccer/match/_/gameId pages** — final scores + xG/possession stats for past fixtures; espn team results pages for last-10 lists.
- **chelseafc.com match reports** — rich detail (scorers, tactical notes, what-comes-next) surfacing via search summaries; same likely for other club official sites.
- **foxsports.com game boxscores** — date-stamped scores for cup fixtures (EFL Cup etc.).
- **aiscore.com / sportsmole H2H pages** — full historical H2H tables with dates and scores.
- **hullcityforum.co.uk threads** — lower-profile club color (lineup debates, match threads) when mainstream coverage is thin.

AH lines for PL favorites are quoted directly (e.g. Chelsea -1.5 @ 1.63); totals may move between 2.75-3.5 — report the range and juice direction.
