For 2026 FIFA World Cup fixtures and match previews, web search results from ESPN, Goal.com, Sports Mole, The Analyst, beIN Sports, Rotowire, Squawka, Sporting News, Khel Now, Soccer Herald, and stadium/event listings provide useful structured data. Direct WebFetch to these domains is often blocked by network/security policies in this environment, so rely on WebSearch summaries.

Useful query patterns:
- "[Team] vs [Team] World Cup 2026 predicted lineups injuries suspensions"
- "[Team] national team last 10 matches [year] results goals scored conceded"
- "[Team] World Cup 2026 group results scores"
- "[Team A] vs [Team B] odds 1X2 Asian handicap over under World Cup 2026"
- "[Team A] vs [Team B] World Cup 2026 referee weather venue"

Odds data often comes from betting aggregators (Oddschecker, Legalbet, Tips.gg, Oddscalendar, TotalFootballAnalysis, BetInAsia) and individual bookmakers (DraftKings, Bovada, Ladbrokes, BetMGM, Unibet, Betfair, William Hill, BetOnline, Australian bookmakers). Treat odds as REPORTED unless cross-referenced with a live bookmaker feed.

Head-to-head data is best cross-referenced across National-Football-Teams, BetVictor, Sports Illustrated, and GiveMeSport because sources differ on whether to count certain friendlies or penalty-shootout outcomes.

Confirmed lineups are typically released ~1 hour before kickoff; earlier listings are PREDICTED. Referee appointments are usually CONFIRMED by FIFA 24-48 hours before the match. Weather forecasts for Atlanta in mid-July show recurring thunderstorm risk; the retractable roof at Mercedes-Benz Stadium mitigates rain but not lightning protocols.
